- Fully adapt to small mobile 320–479px, large mobile 480–767px, tablet 768–1023px, desktop 1024–1439px, and large desktop 1440px+. Use responsive horizontal padding such as `clamp(16px, 4vw, 32px)`. Keep site fast on mobile and slower connections; do not add unnecessary dependencies.
